Exhibition 1: Encuentro (1959)

Varo's Encuentro depicts a solitary figure moving through a narrow, dreamlike space, collecting small human forms from the surroundings. The scene suggests an inner journey of self-discovery, where lost or hidden parts of identity are being reclaimed. With muted tones and precise, surreal detail, the painting evokes a quiet, mystical atmosphere centered on introspection and transformation.

Exhibition 2: Creación de las Aves (1957)

In Creation of the Birds, Remedios Varo depicts a mystical, owl-like figure engaged in the act of creation within a quiet, laboratory-like room. The figure uses a violin, a prism, and starlight to transform light into birds, blending artistic expression with scientific process. This fusion suggests that creativity is both intellectual and spiritual.


The carefully detailed, dreamlike environment reflects Varo’s interest in alchemy, knowledge, and transformation. Through this symbolic scene, the painting explores the idea of the artist as a creator who channels unseen forces into life, emphasizing imagination as a powerful, almost magical act.
