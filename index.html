About the Artist:

Remedios Varo (1908–1963) was a Spanish-born surrealist painter known for her imaginative, dreamlike works that blend science, mysticism, and psychology. She studied at the Real Academia de Bellas Artes de San Fernando in Madrid, where she developed strong technical skills and an early interest in avant-garde movements. During World War II, she fled Europe, eventually settling in Mexico, where she spent the most productive period of her career and became part of a vibrant artistic community.

In Mexico, Varo developed her distinctive, highly detailed style, drawing on influences such as alchemy, esoteric philosophy, and scientific thought. Her paintings often depict solitary figures engaged in mysterious tasks, exploring themes of transformation, inner journeys, and the creative process. Today, her work is widely recognized as a significant contribution to 20th-century surrealism and continues to captivate audiences with its symbolic depth and imaginative vision.
